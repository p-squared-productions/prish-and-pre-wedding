The Meaning of Our Nalangu

Nalangu is a traditional South Indian pre-wedding ceremony held to bless and prepare the bride and groom as they begin their journey towards marriage. It is a joyful occasion that brings both families and loved ones together before the wedding.

At our Nalangu, guests can expect traditional blessings, auspicious rituals, playful games, sweets, music, dancing and plenty of family fun as we celebrate the beginning of our wedding celebrations.
