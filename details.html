The Meaning of a Hindu Wedding

A Hindu wedding is much more than a ceremony—it is a sacred union of two souls, two families, and two journeys becoming one.

Every ritual carries a special meaning and blessing for the couple’s future together.

  • The Sacred Fire (Agni) The fire serves as a divine witness to the marriage and symbolises purity, commitment, and the presence of God.
  • The Tying of the Thali The Thali is tied by the groom around the bride’s neck and symbolises their lifelong commitment, love, and union.
  • Walking Around the Sacred Fire As the couple walks around the sacred fire, they make promises to support one another, build a loving home, and remain faithful partners throughout life.

Together these rituals represent love, trust, unity, prosperity, and a lifelong partnership.
