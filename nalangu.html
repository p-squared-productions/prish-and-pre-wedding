The Meaning of Our Nalangu

Nalangu is a traditional South Indian pre-wedding ceremony held to bless and prepare the bride and groom as they begin their journey towards marriage. It is a joyful occasion that brings both families and loved ones together before the wedding.

The rituals have both auspicious and symbolic meanings.

  • Blessings for the couple Elders and loved ones offer prayers and blessings for a happy, peaceful and prosperous married life.
  • Purification and protection Turmeric, sandalwood and other auspicious items are traditionally used to symbolise purity, protection and the removal of negativity before the wedding.
  • Bringing families together Nalangu creates an opportunity for the bride’s and groom’s families to meet, interact and celebrate as one family.
  • Joy and playfulness The traditional games and playful rituals represent happiness, friendship and the fun that should continue throughout married life.
  • Prosperity and abundance Sweets, gifts and auspicious offerings symbolise wishes for sweetness, good fortune and abundance in the couple’s new life together.
  • Honouring tradition It allows the couple and their families to celebrate the cultural traditions passed down through generations.

At our Nalangu, guests can expect traditional blessings, auspicious rituals, playful games, sweets, music, dancing and plenty of family fun as we celebrate the beginning of our wedding celebrations.
