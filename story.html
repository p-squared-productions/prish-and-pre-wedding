Our Story

A Chance
Encounter

Neither of us was looking for love that evening. Yet somehow, among familiar faces, laughter, and music, our paths crossed for the very first time.

I had recently been reconnecting with old friends when one of my closest friends invited me to a Golden Oldies event at the community centre. She was excited for me to meet her new boyfriend — the man who would soon become her fiancé.

At the very same time, Prenasen was in Johannesburg with absolutely no plans to visit Port Elizabeth. In fact, he didn’t really want to make the trip at all. But his best friend was preparing to propose to his girlfriend and convinced Prenasen to drive down with him for the special occasion.

Neither of us knew it then, but fate was already making plans.

When I arrived at the hall, I immediately went to find my friend. Standing beside her were her boyfriend and his friend, Prenasen. I confidently walked up and introduced myself.

“Hi, I’m Prish.”

The response?

Nothing. Not a single word.

My friend quickly introduced her boyfriend, and without hesitation, I pointed at him and said,

“If you hurt my friend, I’ll kill you.”

Both men looked at me as though I had completely lost my mind.

Mission accomplished.

I walked away and headed straight for the dance floor. What I didn’t realize was that while I was busy dancing, someone was watching.

Prenasen. And he liked what he saw.

A little while later, our paths crossed again at my friend’s birthday party. By then, I had convinced myself that he didn’t like me, so I deliberately didn’t sit next to him. We chatted briefly during the evening, but I still thought nothing of it.

Meanwhile, Prenasen was thinking something very different. As soon as he discovered I was single, he wasted absolutely no time.

A message appeared:

“So I hear you’re single now.”

That one message started a conversation that never ended.

Day after day, message after message, friendship slowly turned into something much more. What began as a reluctant road trip, a protective warning to a stranger, and a chance meeting at a community hall became endless laughter, unwavering support, and a love story neither of us saw coming.

Sometimes the best stories begin when you least expect them. And ours started with a dance, a warning, and a message.

From Port Elizabeth to forever.
